# Product strategy (public summary)

DrivingSupport helps learners rehearse driving-related body movements in a browser using ordinary cameras. It is supplementary material for preparation, repetition, review, and procedural practice.

## Why this approach

Browser delivery lowers setup friction. Body-motion interaction lets practice include actions that keyboard-only prototypes cannot represent. The initial audience is learners who are already studying driving, not people seeking a replacement for licensed instruction.

## Product phases

- MVP: prove one repeatable guided practice slice.
- Curriculum expansion: add carefully scoped skills and content.
- Pilot readiness: improve reliability, consent, support, and operator workflows.
- Instructor tooling: expose useful progress and review information after the learner loop is reliable.

Commercial assumptions, named prospects, pricing, recruitment, and private research remain outside this public repository.
