# DrivingSupport vision

DrivingSupport is a browser-based driving-practice simulator that lets learners rehearse driving-related body movements with ordinary PC or smartphone cameras and no specialized hardware.

The product supports preparation, repetition, review, and procedural practice alongside driving-school instruction. It does not replace real driving instruction or on-road practice.

The early product differentiator is the combination of browser delivery, camera-based body-motion interaction, and Japanese driving-skill education.

## Product principle

Prove repeatable learning value with a small, reliable practice slice before expanding simulation realism or curriculum breadth.

## Privacy principle

Prefer camera → browser-side processing → landmarks/derived state → driving logic → score. The product must document actual data behavior and must not claim raw video is local-only unless that is verified.
